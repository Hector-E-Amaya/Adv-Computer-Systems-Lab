## 7. Misleading setups

**Buffered vs direct** (4 KiB random read, QD 1, same 256 MiB file; buffered = psync after a warming sequential read with invalidate=0):

| rep | direct IOPS | direct mean us | buffered IOPS | buffered mean us | warm-pass MB/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7960.0 | 124.7 | 176926.0 | 5.4 | 5063.3 |
| 2 | 7326.8 | 135.6 | 54100.5 | 18.1 | 5112.6 |
| 3 | 5252.3 | 189.1 | 168842.8 | 5.7 | 4670.6 |

Median gap 23x. Warm-pass reads at 4671-5113 MB/s exceed what the SSD delivers (~0.75 GB/s here): they came from RAM.
Archived first run (pitfall_run1): buffered IOPS 187.1K, 116.6K, 2.5K; rep 3's warm pass read from disk and the buffered reads missed the cache. The same command swung from page-cache to slower-than-direct results with OS cache state.
